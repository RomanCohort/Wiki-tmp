Education Design Board

Begin with what learning needs. Build a complementary portfolio within the resources you have.

Learning needs→Real-world conditions→Complementary formats
01

What should learning enable?

Choose the emphasis for this context. All three functions can coexist.

Cognitive

Understand concepts, mechanisms, judgement and design.

Affective

Connect knowledge with experience, relationships, attitudes and values.

Experiential

Learn through operation, choice, experimentation, cooperation and creation.

02

What can this context support?

Keep the conditions in place; adjust the resources to reality.

Required conditions

Required conditions, not optional trade-offs.
  • ○
    Scientific Accuracy

    Check explanations and the limits of analogies.

  • ○
    Safety

    Plan appropriate guidance and safe participation.

  • ○
    Privacy

    Protect personal information and participant choice.

  • ○
    Accessibility

    Provide suitable language, access and alternatives.

Meet all four before comparing or implementing a portfolio.

Available resources

Work with what you have.
Include setup, participation and reflection.
Team size does not imply specialist training.
Digital formats need a suitable device.
Choose where participation will happen.

From functions to portfolio

Suggested Portfolio

A small set of formats with different jobs.
Complementarity matters more than quantity.

What belongs together?

Set your learning emphasis and resources, then explore a starting combination.

    Why this combination?

    Qualitative design guidance, not measured outcomes or a unique optimum. Adapt the formats to your learners.

    Suggestions assume all four required conditions can be met.
    This board does not verify implementation readiness.
